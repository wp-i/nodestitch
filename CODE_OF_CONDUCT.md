# Code of conduct

Nodestitch contributors are expected to be respectful, constructive, and
welcoming. Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, threats, and the
publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Keep technical discussion focused on the work. Maintainers may edit or remove
harmful content and may restrict participation when behavior repeatedly harms
the project or its contributors.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers rather than in
a public issue. Maintainers should handle reports confidentially and avoid
conflicts of interest when reviewing them.
